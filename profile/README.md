# PanganKita 🌱

PanganKita is an Indonesian technology and research initiative focused on building practical digital solutions for a more efficient, accessible, and sustainable food ecosystem.

We are developing tools that help food businesses give eligible unsold food another chance by connecting it with nearby consumers who are looking for affordable food options.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for food to retain its value instead of being unnecessarily discarded.

## What We Focus On

- Building reliable and accessible digital products
- Helping food businesses recover value from eligible unsold food
- Connecting nearby consumers with more affordable food options
- Supporting responsible food handling and transparent marketplace practices
- Measuring real product and environmental impact
- Exploring research-driven approaches to time-sensitive food matching
- Building software with strong engineering, testing, accessibility, and open-source standards

## Our Approach

PanganKita is not intended to be just another food marketplace or environmental-awareness application.

We focus on creating a practical system where:

**Food businesses identify eligible unsold food  
→ nearby consumers discover it  
→ reservations are made within a limited pickup window  
→ food is successfully collected  
→ the outcome is measured**

Over time, PanganKita also serves as a platform for research into how factors such as distance, remaining pickup time, availability, and demand can improve the successful redistribution of time-sensitive food.
